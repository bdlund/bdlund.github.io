I study how artificial intelligence changes libraries, information seeking, and scholarly communication. This site gathers my recent papers on AI literacy, AI ethics and policy, privacy, and information behavior, each with its own page, abstract, and downloadable PDF.

I direct the Computational Humanities and Innovative Literacies Lab (CHILL) at UNT. My work uses surveys, content analysis, and conceptual analysis to understand how people and institutions respond to generative AI.

CHILL affiliate and frequent co-author Abbie Teel shares her research, teaching, and service in her online portfolio.
