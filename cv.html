Curriculum vitae

Brady D. Lund is Assistant Professor of Information Science at the University of North Texas, where he directs the Computational Humanities and Innovative Literacies Lab (CHILL). His research covers AI literacy, AI ethics and policy, explainable AI, and information behavior. He earned his PhD at Emporia State University in 2022.
