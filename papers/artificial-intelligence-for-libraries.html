About the book

Artificial Intelligence for Libraries explains what AI is, how it works, why it matters to all types of libraries, and how it can be used. It covers AI in library systems and in teaching and learning, and helps readers recognize ethical issues raised by the technology. Its ten chapters cover the basics of AI and how it works, AI ethics, the history of AI and libraries, library systems, AI literacy, teaching and learning, scholarly publishing, building AI tools for libraries, and the next generation of AI libraries. The final chapter is a practical guide to using different types of AI in libraries. This page offers the book description, table of contents, chapter abstracts, and reference list; the full text is available from the publisher.

Description

Artificial intelligence is an extremely important but little-understood topic, including in libraries. This timely book explores what AI is, how it works, how it’s relevant to all types of libraries, and how it can be used. Readers will better understand the use of AI in library systems and applying AI to teaching and learning environments, and they will grow in the ability to discern potential ethical issues posed by this emerging technology. The final chapter provides a practical guide to using various types of AI in libraries.
