Ethnographic research: Example of a possible process

What the figure shows

The figure shows one possible sequence for ethnographic research. The researcher starts with a distinct population, selects a subset for naturalistic observation and interaction, and interprets what is observed by asking what the behavior means and why participants value it. The work then narrows to interviews with a smaller group and to archival research. It was made for teaching research methods.

Abstract

This figure shows common steps and approaches used in ethnographic research. A researcher starts from a distinct population and selects a subset for naturalistic observation and interaction. The researcher interprets what is observed by asking what the behavior means, how participants are experiencing it, and why they value it. The process then narrows to interviews with a smaller group of participants and to archival research. The figure was prepared for the book chapter "Diversification of Research Methods in Information Science" (Lund, 2024) and is shared for teaching and research methods instruction.
